VoltUnplugged autopost: Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

VoltUnplugged autopost ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner, the creator of the VoltUnplugged channels, to upload the owner's own original videos to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts on a schedule. It has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses, uses and stores

  • Google / YouTube: an OAuth refresh token with the youtube.upload scope, used only to upload the owner's videos (title, description, tags, privacy status) to the owner's channel. The app does not read, collect or store any other YouTube data, viewer data or channel analytics.
  • TikTok and Instagram: OAuth tokens and the account ID and username, used only to upload the owner's videos to those accounts.

How data is stored and shared

Tokens are stored encrypted in the owner's private GitHub Actions secrets and in a local file on the owner's computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No cookies or tracking are used by the app or this website.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page, and in the TikTok and Instagram app settings. Stored tokens are deleted within 7 days of revocation or on request, and are refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days as part of normal operation.
