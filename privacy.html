Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts.

  • Data accessed: the OAuth tokens needed to upload videos, and basic account info (account ID and username) to confirm which account is connected.
  • Data is stored only in the owner's private, encrypted storage and is never sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • No data about other users or viewers is collected.
  • To revoke access at any time, remove the app in your Google, TikTok or Instagram account settings. Stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact: via the GitHub repository that hosts this page.
